Un needs no account, login or demo credentials.

To try a lesson:
1. Launch the app and allow the camera, the microphone and speech recognition.
2. Add a child (any name) and tap Continue to start the first adventure.
3. Point the camera at a table and put one to three small objects (toys, cups, fruit) in front of it. The tutor numbers them on the screen and asks, out loud, how many there are.
4. Answer by voice ("three") or with the number buttons.

Notes for review:
- Toy recognition runs on the device (an on-device model); no camera frames or audio are stored or sent anywhere by the app.
- Speech recognition uses Apple's Speech framework, on the device where supported.
- No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking. The children's names and progress stay on the device.
- On a Mac, the camera is the one chosen in Settings (default: the system's camera); an iPhone as a Continuity Camera works best, looking down at the table.
