Un teaches early maths with the toys your child already has. Put a few toys on the table, point the phone's camera at them, and a friendly tutor takes it from there: it sees the toys, numbers each one on the screen, asks questions out loud and listens to the answer.

LEARN WITH REAL THINGS
Counting, adding and taking away happen with toys the child can touch and move. The camera recognises them and follows along as the child adds one, takes one away or splits them into groups.

A TUTOR THAT TALKS AND LISTENS
Every question is spoken aloud and the child answers by voice. If the microphone can't hear, big number buttons appear instead.

TEN ADVENTURES
1. Three passengers: counting to 3
2. Picnic baskets: adding to 5
3. Cave train: taking away
4. Bridge builders: comparing
5. Parcel detectives: numbers and amounts
6. Firefly gates: counting to 10
7. Supply launch: adding to 10
8. Homeward trail: taking away to 10
9. Ten-seat ferry: making ten
10. Fair-share cafe: sharing equally

FOR EVERY CHILD IN THE FAMILY
Add each child by name. The tutor greets them, remembers where they stopped and picks up from there next time. Progress is shown as a trail of adventures, and any adventure can be started at any time.

ENGLISH AND RUSSIAN
Switch the tutor's language with one tap, even in the middle of a lesson.

CALM BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases. Toy recognition runs on the phone itself, and the children's names and progress stay on this device.
